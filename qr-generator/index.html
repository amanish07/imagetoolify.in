Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is this QR Code Generator completely free for commercial and personal use?
Yes, our QR Code Generator on imagetoolify.in is 100% free with no hidden subscription costs, tiered pricing, trial periods, or account creation requirements. You are free to generate an unlimited volume of QR codes for personal projects, commercial brand campaigns, retail packaging, print ads, and digital media.
2. Do the generated QR codes ever expire or have scan limits?
No, all QR codes generated through this tool are completely static. Static QR codes encode the target data (such as web links, plain text, UPI strings, or contact cards) directly into the geometric matrix itself. Because there is no intermediary redirection server hosting your data, the codes will never expire, run out of scans, or stop functioning.
3. What is the difference between static and dynamic QR codes?
Static QR codes store destination data permanently inside the code matrix pattern. Once generated, the encoded content cannot be edited, but the code remains active forever without server dependencies. Dynamic QR codes store a short redirect link that points to a server where the destination URL can be modified later and scan metrics tracked. Our generator creates privacy-friendly, zero-cost static QR codes.
4. Why is adding a white border or margin slider crucial for printing?
Every standard QR reader requires a clear perimeter known as a "Quiet Zone" to isolate the code pattern from surrounding visuals, text, or dark backgrounds. Without an adequate white margin, camera sensors struggle to detect code boundaries, resulting in failed scans. Adding a margin of 15px–25px ensures seamless readability across printed flyers, packaging, and digital screens.
5. How does selecting a country code work for WhatsApp, Call, and SMS types?
International telecommunication standards require full country prefixes to route calls and messaging protocols correctly across borders. By selecting your country code (e.g., +91 for India, +1 for USA, +44 for UK) from our dropdown, our script formats the telephone string into global uniform URI protocols like tel:+919876543210 or https://wa.me/919876543210, preventing local dialing format errors globally.
6. Will uploading a center logo reduce the scanning reliability of my QR code?
No, QR codes utilize Reed-Solomon Error Correction algorithms designed to withstand partial surface damage or overlay obstruction up to 30%. Our rendering engine automatically applies high error correction levels (Level H) when a custom logo is uploaded, allowing smartphones to read the surrounding matrix modules effortlessly without data corruption.
7. What image file formats are supported for custom logo uploads?
Our custom logo engine accepts standard web raster image formats including PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, and SVG. For best visual results, we recommend uploading a high-resolution square logo image (e.g., 500x500 pixels) with a transparent PNG background so it renders cleanly in the central canvas box without harsh background edges.
8. Is my personal input data or uploaded logo saved on your servers?
No. We prioritize absolute user privacy. All QR code matrix calculations, dynamic form rendering, canvas graphics drawing, and logo processing occur 100% client-side inside your local web browser session via JavaScript. No contact numbers, text entries, location coordinates, UPI keys, or uploaded graphic files are ever uploaded, recorded, or saved to our servers.
9. How do I generate a WiFi QR code so guests can connect automatically?
Select the 'WiFi' tab in the top selection grid, enter your exact Network Name (SSID), type in the network Wi-Fi password, and select your security encryption type (WPA/WPA2 is standard for most routers). When guests scan the generated QR code with their mobile camera, iOS and Android devices will prompt them to join the network automatically without typing the password.
10. How does the UPI Payment QR code generator work for Indian users?
Select the 'UPI Pay' tab, type in your valid Virtual Payment Address or UPI ID (e.g., username@upi, mobile@paytm, or name@okicici), enter the Payee/Merchant name, and optionally specify a fixed transfer amount. When scanned by payment apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM, it opens the payment screen with your pre-filled details for instant settlements.
11. What is a vCard QR code and how is it used on business cards?
A vCard QR code encodes digital contact card information following the standard electronic business card protocol. When scanned by a mobile phone camera, it allows the user to save your full contact details—including full name, phone number, work email address, and company organization name—directly into their smartphone address book with a single tap.
12. How do color choices affect QR code scannability?
QR code optical sensors rely on high light contrast to distinguish foreground dark data blocks from background light spaces. Always choose a dark color (e.g., black, deep blue, dark grey, navy) for the foreground QR pattern and a light color (e.g., white, off-white, light yellow) for the background. Never invert colors (e.g., white dots on a black background), as many legacy camera readers cannot parse inverted matrices.
13. What dot pattern options are available and do they affect performance?
We offer four dot matrix aesthetics: Square, Rounded, Dots, and Classy. All four dot styles are fully compliant with standard QR ISO reading specs. While aesthetic shapes change the visual design of the matrix modules, they do not impact scanner compatibility across modern iOS, Android, or hardware barcode scanners.
14. What size should a printed QR code be for reliable scanning?
For small items like business cards, product tags, or table stands, the printed QR code size should be at least 2 cm x 2 cm (0.8 in x 0.8 in). For larger items like posters, billboards, or wall banners, follow the standard 10:1 scanning ratio rule: the distance between the scanner and the code divided by 10 gives the minimum physical width of the printed QR code (e.g., a scan distance of 10 feet requires a 1-foot wide QR code).
15. What format is the downloaded QR code image and is it print-ready?
Our generator exports high-definition PNG (Portable Network Graphics) images. PNG is a lossless raster format that preserves crisp vector-like dot edges without pixel blur or compression artifacts, making it ideal for both online digital graphics and high-resolution physical print media.

18. Why is my generated QR code not scanning properly?
If your QR code fails to scan, check these common causes: (1) Low color contrast between foreground dots and background color; (2) The white margin border slider is set to 0px; (3) The center logo image uploaded is too large and covering corner anchor squares; (4) The encoded target URL contains typos or missing protocols; or (5) Excessive data density packed into a small physical print size.

If you need to update information, simply generate a new QR code or use a short link domain that you control as your destination URL.

23. What are the three large square markers in the corners of the QR code?
The three distinct square patterns situated in the top-left, top-right, and bottom-left corners are called Position Detection Patterns or Finder Patterns. Optical camera sensors use these landmarks to recognize the orientation, scale, angle, and tilt of the QR code matrix relative to the lens before reading the interior data dots.
24. What is the maximum character capacity of a static QR code?
Static QR codes can theoretically store up to 7,089 numeric characters, 4,296 alphanumeric characters, or 2,953 binary bytes. However, encoding extremely long URLs or paragraphs creates an extremely dense visual matrix with tiny modules that are harder for phone cameras to scan. Keeping target text concise ensures higher scannability.
