About QR Code Generator

The imagetoolify QR Code Generator is a free online tool that allows users to create QR codes instantly for text, links and other information. QR codes are widely used to share websites, contact details and digital content quickly using a smartphone camera.

This tool generates high quality QR codes directly inside your browser without uploading any data to a server. You can also upload a logo to place it in the center of the QR code and download the final image with a clean white border for better scanning and printing.

How to Use QR Code Generator

  1. Enter the text or website link in the input field.
  2. The QR code will automatically generate while typing.
  3. Upload a logo image if you want to place it in the center of the QR code.
  4. Preview the QR code instantly.
  5. Click the Download button to save the QR code image.
