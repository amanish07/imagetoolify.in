Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

At imagetoolify, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our image, PDF, and QR code tools.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. All images, PDFs, and QR code data are processed directly in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers.

  • No image, PDF, or QR data is stored.
  • No user registration or login is required.
  • Personal details like name or email are collected only when you contact us.

Cookies & Advertising

We may use cookies for analytics and advertising purposes such as Google AdSense. Cookies help us improve user experience and show relevant ads.

Push Notifications

imagetoolify uses OneSignal to send browser push notifications. These notifications may include updates, new tools, and important announcements. OneSignal may collect non-personal information such as browser type, device information, and subscription status to deliver notifications properly. You can unsubscribe from notifications anytime using your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services like Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and OneSignal may collect non-personal data according to their own privacy policies.

Children’s Information

imagetoolify does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
