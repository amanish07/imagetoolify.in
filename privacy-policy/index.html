Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 20 August 2026

At imagetoolify, accessible from https://imagetoolify.in/, user privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information handled when you interact with our web tools and services.

1. Client-Side File Processing & Data Security

We do not collect, upload, store, or view your uploaded media files or personal documents. All operations—including document photo resizing, circle image cropping, image format conversion, PDF creation, YouTube thumbnail fetching, and QR code generation—are performed directly within your local client browser using web APIs.

  • No File Uploads: Your images, PDFs, signatures, and personal assets never touch or upload to external servers.
  • No Account Required: You can access all utilities anonymously without creating an account or providing personal identification.
  • Direct Inquiries: Personal details such as your name or email address are collected strictly when you voluntarily reach out to us via our Contact form.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like most standard websites, imagetoolify uses log files and performance tracking services (such as Google Analytics). These logs collect non-personal data including internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts to analyze user trends, administer the site, and improve site load speeds.

3. Cookies & Advertising

imagetoolify may use cookies to store user preferences and optimize browsing experiences. Third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, use cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve relevant advertisements to visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Users may choose to decline or customize cookie settings through their individual web browser options.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

imagetoolify's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or external websites. We encourage users to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers (like Google AdSense) for more detailed information regarding their practices and instructions on how to opt-out.

5. Children’s Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

imagetoolify does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.
