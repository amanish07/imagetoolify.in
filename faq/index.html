Frequently Asked Questions

What is imagetoolify? ▼
imagetoolify is a free online platform that provides image, PDF and QR code tools that work directly in your browser.
Is imagetoolify free to use? ▼
Yes, all tools on imagetoolify are completely free.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
No, you can use imagetoolify without signup or login.
Are my files uploaded to any server? ▼
No. All processing happens locally in your browser.
Can I resize images for PAN Card? ▼
Yes, you can resize images for PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport and other documents.
Can I reduce image size in KB? ▼
Yes, imagetoolify allows you to compress and reduce image size in KB.
Which image formats are supported? ▼
JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP formats are supported.
Can I convert image to PDF? ▼
Yes, you can convert images to PDF using imagetoolify tools.
Can I convert PDF to image? ▼
Yes, imagetoolify provides PDF to image conversion tools.
Is QR code generator available? ▼
Yes, imagetoolify includes a QR code generator.
Can I add logo to QR code? ▼
Yes, you can upload a custom logo inside the QR code.
Does imagetoolify work on mobile? ▼
Yes, imagetoolify is fully mobile friendly.
Is imagetoolify safe to use? ▼
Yes, your files never leave your device.
Can I use imagetoolify for government forms? ▼
Yes, tools are useful for PAN, Aadhaar, passport and other forms.
Do I need to install any software? ▼
No, all tools work online in your browser.
