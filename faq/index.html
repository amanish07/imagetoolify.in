Frequently Asked Questions

Last Updated: 20 August 2026

What is imagetoolify?
imagetoolify is a free online platform that provides image, PDF, YouTube thumbnail downloading, and QR code tools that work directly in your browser.
Is imagetoolify free to use?
Yes, all tools on imagetoolify are completely free for unlimited usage.
Do I need to create an account?
No, you can use all imagetoolify tools instantly without signup, registration, or login.
Are my files uploaded to any server?
No. All file processing happens locally directly in your web browser, ensuring complete privacy and data security.
Can I resize images for PAN Card and other official documents?
Yes, you can resize photos and signatures in KB for PAN card, voter ID, passport applications, and other government forms.
Can I reduce image size in KB?
Yes, imagetoolify allows you to easily compress and reduce image file sizes in KB or MB without sacrificing quality.
Which image formats are supported?
Popular image formats such as JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP are fully supported for conversion and compression.
Can I convert images to PDF?
Yes, you can convert single or multiple images into a clean PDF document using our PDF tools.
Can I convert PDF files to images?
Yes, imagetoolify offers quick tools to extract images from PDF files.
Can I download YouTube video thumbnails using imagetoolify?
Yes, using our YouTube Thumbnail Downloader, you can instantly extract cover images in HD, 1080p, or 4K quality by simply pasting a video URL.
Can I copy YouTube video titles and channel names?
Yes, our YouTube Thumbnail Downloader also automatically fetches video metadata, allowing you to copy the title and channel name in a single click.
Is a QR code generator available?
Yes, imagetoolify includes a versatile QR code generator for URLs, text, Wi-Fi details, and contact links.
Can I add a custom logo to a generated QR code?
Yes, you can easily upload a custom brand or personal logo to display at the center of your QR code.
Does imagetoolify work on mobile phones?
Yes, imagetoolify is fully responsive and optimized for seamless use on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
Is imagetoolify safe and secure?
Yes, because processing happens locally on your client browser, your files and links never get stored or tracked.
Do I need to install any browser extensions or software?
No, all tools work entirely online through standard web browsers without requiring software installation.
What is the Circle Image Cropper tool?
The Circle Image Cropper allows you to easily crop any image into a perfect round/circular shape, ideal for social media profile pictures, avatars, and badges.
Can I export cropped circular images with a transparent background?
Yes, when saving circular images as PNG format, the outer area automatically becomes transparent, making it perfect for UI designs and web graphics.
Are there any restrictions on file size for uploads?
Since processing happens directly on your device using client-side Web APIs, there are practically no strict file size limits beyond your browser's memory capacity.
Does processing images reduce their original resolution?
No, unless you explicitly choose to compress or resize dimensions, imagetoolify retains the original resolution and sharpness of your uploaded files.
Can I process multiple images at once?
Yes, our conversion and resizing tools support batch processing, allowing you to select and edit multiple photos simultaneously to save time.
How do I download high-quality YouTube Shorts thumbnails?
Simply copy the link of any YouTube Shorts video and paste it into our YouTube Thumbnail Downloader to extract its thumbnail in maximum resolution.
Can I use imagetoolify tools without an internet connection?
No, you cannot use imagetoolify without an active internet connection. An internet connection is required to access and load the website tools.
Is there any watermark added to processed files or QR codes?
No, imagetoolify never adds any watermarks, branding, or logos to your edited photos, PDFs, or generated QR codes.
What vector or custom formats can I export QR codes into?
You can export your generated QR codes in clean image formats like PNG and JPG, ready for print media, business cards, or online sharing.
Who should I contact if I face an issue or want to report a bug?
You can reach out to us directly through our Contact page or send us a message with details about the issue, and our team will resolve it promptly.
