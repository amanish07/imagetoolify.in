Where and How to Use This Tool for Govt Documents

This utility is designed for instant browser-based processing, enabling you to format your scanned files specifically for the following requirements and portals:

For Voter ID Photo & Signature Resizing

Where to use: National Voters' Service Portal (NVSP) and the official ECI Voter Service Portal during Form 6, Form 6B, or Form 8 submissions.

How to use: Upload your passport photograph and set dimensions to 350 x 450 pixels (under 50 KB). For signatures or thumb impressions, crop tightly and set dimensions to 300 x 120 pixels (under 20 KB) in JPEG format to prevent portal validation errors.

For PAN Card Photo & Signature Resizing

Where to use: NSDL (Protean) and UTIITSL portals for new PAN card applications or demographic changes.

How to use: Format your photograph to exactly 295 x 413 pixels with a maximum size of 30 KB. Crop signatures into a 2:1 aspect ratio at 300 x 150 pixels keeping the file weight under 10 KB in strict JPEG format.

For All Government Websites, Photo & Document Resizing

Where to use: State gateways like RTPS Bihar (for income, caste, and residential certificates), employment boards (UPSC, SSC, banking exams), and scholarship portals.

How to use: Upload bulky PDF document scans (up to several megabytes) and compress or convert them into single-page optimized files under 200 KB to 300 KB. This ensures instant upload success without triggering server-side script failures or dimension mismatches.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What are the exact PAN Card photo, signature, and document specifications?
PAN Card photos must be 295 x 413 px (Max 30 KB), signatures 300 x 150 px (Max 10 KB) in JPEG format, and support documents (Identity, Address, DOB proof) must be under 300 KB.
2. What are the official Voter ID Card (NVSP Portal) requirements?
Voter ID photos must be 350 x 450 px (Max 50 KB), signatures / thumb impressions 300 x 120 px (Max 20 KB), and age/address proof documents should be under 200 KB.
3. How does cropping help before resizing?
Cropping removes unnecessary borders and margins, ensuring your photo or document scan fits clearly within official portal upload specifications.
4. Are my uploaded files stored on your server?
100% private and safe. All cropping, resizing, and compression happen entirely within your browser memory. No file is ever stored on external servers.
5. Why do government portals reject photos even when they are in JPG format?
Portals often reject images due to incorrect pixel dimensions, color profile mismatches (such as CMYK instead of RGB), or exceeding the strict byte size limit despite acceptable dimensions.
6. How can I reduce a PDF document size from 2 MB to under 300 KB for RTPS uploads?
You can use a browser-based PDF compressor or convert the PDF pages into optimized grayscale images and recompile them into a compressed single-page PDF document.
7. Is it acceptable to wear spectacles in a PAN card or Voter ID photo?
Clear prescription glasses without heavy frames or reflective glare are generally accepted, but sunglasses, tinted lenses, or glasses causing flash reflection will lead to application rejection.
8. What is the difference between image dimensions (pixels) and file size (KB)?
Dimensions refer to the width and height in pixels, while file size refers to the digital storage weight determined by image compression quality and color depth.
9. Can I upload a signature photographed with a smartphone camera?
Yes, provided the photo is taken under bright natural light on plain white paper, cropped tightly to remove shadows, and converted to high-contrast JPEG within the allowed byte limit.
10. What should I do if the NSDL portal shows an "Invalid Image Format" error?
Ensure the file extension is strictly .jpg or .jpeg (avoiding .png, .webp, or uppercase extensions like .JPG) and that the file is saved in standard RGB color space.
11. Are thumb impressions legally valid substitutes for signatures on government forms?
Yes, illiterate applicants or individuals unable to sign can upload a scanned thumb impression within the designated signature dimension box per specific form guidelines.
12. How do I handle multi-page documents when the portal allows only a single file upload?
Merge multiple pages into a single compressed PDF file or combine the pages into a vertical image collage before uploading.
13. Why does my resized image look blurry after compression?
Over-compressing an image to meet an extremely low file size limit strips away pixel data, causing blurriness. Start with a clean, high-resolution crop and lower the JPEG quality slider gradually.
14. What background color is mandatory for Indian passport and identity photographs?
A plain white or off-white background is universally required by Indian government portals and official documentation systems.
15. Can I use PNG format for uploading documents to government portals?
Most Indian government portals strictly mandate JPEG for photos/signatures and PDF for documents; PNG files are frequently blocked or cause upload script failures.
16. How do I check the exact pixel dimensions of an image on Windows or Mac?
Hover your mouse over the image file icon in your file explorer, or right-click and select Properties (Windows) or Get Info (Mac) to view dimensions and file size.
17. What is the ideal scanning resolution (DPI) for document uploads?
A setting of 150 to 200 DPI is optimal, providing crisp text readability while keeping the final PDF file size well below typical 200 KB to 300 KB ceilings.
18. Why do some portal upload buttons remain disabled after selecting a file?
This usually occurs when the selected file exceeds the maximum permitted byte size or violates strict naming conventions by containing special characters or spaces.
19. How can I ensure my signature background remains pure white?
Use a threshold or brightness adjustment tool to eliminate grey shadows cast by smartphone lighting before saving the signature as a JPEG.
20. Do state portal requirements (like RTPS Bihar) differ from central portals like NSDL?
While central portals have standardized automated validators, state portals often feature legacy upload scripts that exhibit strict file size enforcement without detailed error readouts.
21. What happens if my identity document photo is older than 6 months?
Portals do not verify the exact age of the photo automatically, but significant mismatch with your current appearance can cause verification delays during physical or biometric checks.
22. Is client-side browser processing safe for sensitive government identity documents?
Yes, because processing occurs locally in your browser sandbox using JavaScript, your documents never transit across or get logged on remote servers.
23. How do I fix orientation issues if my scanned document appears sideways?
Rotate the image by 90-degree increments in a local editor or browser preview tool before applying the crop and resize preset.
24. What are the formatting rules for official stamps or seal integrations?
Stamps and seals must be scanned in color at 200 DPI and cropped tightly to ensure they fit secondary document verification boxes on application forms.
25. How do I clear browser cache if my image resizer tool fails to load updated scripts?
Perform a hard refresh (Ctrl + F5 on Windows or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac) to reload the web utility cleanly and resume document formatting.
