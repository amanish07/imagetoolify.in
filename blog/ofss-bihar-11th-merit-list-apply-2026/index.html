Complete Guide: How to Apply for 11th Merit List Through OFSS Bihar in 2026

Posted on: 15 April 2026

If you are a student in Bihar who has recently passed Class 10 and is now planning to take admission in Class 11 (Intermediate), then understanding the OFSS Bihar system is extremely important for you. The admission process in Bihar is not done offline anymore—it is fully digital and centralized through the OFSS Bihar platform.

This detailed guide will help you understand everything about the 11th merit list, how to apply, how selection works, how to check your result, and what to do after selection. Whether you are applying for Science, Commerce, or Arts, this blog will cover all steps in a simple and clear way.

📌 What is OFSS Bihar?

OFSS stands for Online Facilitation System for Students, which is managed by the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB). It is an online system designed to simplify the admission process for Intermediate (Class 11) in Bihar.

Earlier, students had to visit multiple colleges and fill separate forms, which was time-consuming and confusing. But now, through OFSS, you can apply to multiple schools and colleges using a single form.

👉 Official Website: https://ofssbihar.net

Through this platform, students can:

  • Fill admission form online
  • Choose multiple colleges
  • Select stream (Science/Commerce/Arts)
  • Check merit list
  • Download intimation letter
  • Complete admission process

🎯 What is the 11th Merit List?

The 11th Merit List is a selection list released by OFSS Bihar that determines which students get admission into which colleges or schools.

The selection is based on:

  • Class 10 marks
  • Reservation category
  • College preferences

There are usually multiple rounds of merit lists:

  1. First Merit List
  2. Second Merit List
  3. Third Merit List
  4. Spot Admission

Each merit list fills available seats step by step.

🖥️ Step-by-Step Process to Apply for 11th Merit List

Now let’s understand the full process in detail.

🔹 Step 1: Visit Official Website

Go to the official OFSS portal: 👉 https://ofssbihar.net

This is the only valid website for application. Avoid fake or duplicate sites.

🔹 Step 2: Click on CAF (Common Application Form)

On the homepage:

  • Click on Common Application Form (CAF)
  • This is the main form used for admission

CAF allows you to apply to multiple colleges at once.

🔹 Step 3: Registration Process

You need to register first:

  • Enter your name
  • Mobile number
  • Email ID

After entering details:

  • You will receive an OTP
  • Verify your number

🔹 Step 4: Fill Personal Details

Now fill your personal information:

  • Name (as per marksheet)
  • Date of Birth
  • Gender
  • Category (General/SC/ST/OBC/EWS)
  • Address

Make sure all details are correct.

🔹 Step 5: Academic Details

Enter your Class 10 details:

  • Roll Code
  • Roll Number
  • Board Name
  • Marks obtained

These details are very important because they decide your merit ranking.

🔹 Step 6: Select Colleges and Schools

This is one of the most important steps.

You can choose up to 20 colleges/schools.

Tips:

  • Choose nearby colleges
  • Select reputed institutions
  • Fill maximum options
  • Arrange preferences carefully

🔹 Step 7: Choose Stream

Select your desired stream:

  • Science
  • Commerce
  • Arts

Tip:

Choose stream based on your interest and marks.

🔹 Step 8: Upload Photo and Signature

Upload:

  • Passport size photo
  • Signature

Make sure images are clear and as per size guidelines.

🔹 Step 9: Pay Application Fee

Application fee is around ₹350.

Payment Options:

  • Debit Card
  • Credit Card
  • Net Banking
  • E-Challan

🔹 Step 10: Final Submission

Before submitting:

  • Check all details carefully
  • Make corrections if needed

Then:

  • Click Submit
  • Download acknowledgment receipt

📊 How Merit List is Prepared?

The merit list is created using the following factors:

1. Marks in Class 10

Higher marks increase your chances.

2. Reservation Policy

Reserved categories get benefits.

3. College Preference

Your selected choices matter.

📥 How to Check 11th Merit List

Follow these steps:

  1. Visit https://ofssbihar.net
  2. Click on “Merit List / Intimation Letter”
  3. Enter:
    • Application Reference Number
    • Mobile Number
  4. Click Submit

You will see:

  • Selected college
  • Stream
  • Admission details

📄 What is Intimation Letter?

The Intimation Letter is a very important document.

It includes:

  • Your name
  • Selected college
  • Stream
  • Admission deadline

You must download and print it.

🏫 Admission Process After Selection

After selection:

Step 1:

Visit the allotted college

Step 2:

Carry documents:

  • Intimation Letter
  • Marksheet
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Photos

Step 3:

Pay admission fee

Step 4:

Complete verification

🔄 Slide-Up Process Explained

If you are not satisfied with your college:

You can apply for Slide-Up.

What happens?

  • You may get a better college in next list
  • Your current seat is upgraded

❌ What If You Are Not Selected?

If your name is not in merit list:

  • Wait for next list
  • Apply for spot admission

Do not panic—many students get admission in later rounds.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

Many students make mistakes while applying. Avoid these:

  • Filling wrong marks
  • Selecting random colleges
  • Not checking merit list
  • Missing deadlines

💡 Tips to Increase Selection Chances

  • Fill maximum college options
  • Choose realistic colleges
  • Enter correct marks
  • Apply early
  • Keep checking updates

📅 Important Dates (General Timeline)

  • Application Start: June–July
  • First Merit List: July
  • Second Merit List: August
  • Third Merit List: August
  • Spot Admission: September

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Can I apply offline?

No, the process is fully online.

Q2: How many colleges can I choose?

Up to 20.

Q3: Can I change stream later?

Only through official process.

Q4: What if I miss admission?

Your seat may be cancelled.
