PAN Card Aakhir Chahiye Kisliye?

PAN ka full form Permanent Account Number hai. Ye Income Tax Department ki taraf se issue kiya jane wala unique number hai, jo tax aur financial records mein kisi person ya eligible entity ki identification mein kaam aata hai.

PAN ko sirf “Income Tax wala card” samajhna thoda incomplete hoga.

Aapko PAN ki zarurat alag-alag situations mein pad sakti hai. Jaise bank aur financial services se jude kuch kaam, investments, certain high-value transactions, tax-related work, property transactions aur business activities.

Har transaction mein PAN compulsory ho, ye zaroori nahi hai. Lekin jahan PAN required hai, wahan bina PAN ke kaam atak sakta hai.

Isi wajah se bahut se log income tax return file na karne ke bawajood PAN banwate hain.

Kya PAN Card Banwane Ke Liye Office Jana Padta Hai?

Nahi.

Aapke paas agar smartphone aur internet connection hai, to kai PAN-related services online access ki ja sakti hain.

Aapki situation ke hisaab se do options sabse important hain:

Instant e-PAN aur Regular PAN Application.

Agar aap eligible hain aur aapke paas Aadhaar aur Aadhaar-linked mobile number hai, to Instant e-PAN ka option kaafi convenient hai.

Agar aapko physical PAN Card chahiye, ya Instant e-PAN ki eligibility aap par apply nahi hoti, to regular PAN application kar sakte hain.

Instant e-PAN Aur Physical PAN Mein Kya Difference Hai?

Yahin par sabse zyada confusion hota hai.

Instant e-PAN

Isme aapko electronic PAN milta hai. Aadhaar-based verification hota hai aur eligible applicants ke liye service free hai.

Agar aapko bas PAN number chahiye aur digital copy se kaam chal jayega, to ye convenient option hai.

Physical PAN Card

Isme PAN ki printed card copy bhi mil sakti hai. Iske liye regular application ya applicable physical-card/reprint service use ki ja sakti hai aur charges lag sakte hain.

Simple words mein:

Sirf digital PAN chahiye → Instant e-PAN dekhein.

Printed card bhi chahiye → Regular/physical PAN option dekhein.

Aadhaar Se Instant e-PAN Kaise Banaye?

Agar aap eligible hain, to Instant e-PAN ka process kaafi straightforward hai.

Sabse pehle Income Tax Department ke e-Filing portal par jaakar Instant e-PAN service open karein.

Wahan new e-PAN ke liye option select karein.

Uske baad Aadhaar number enter karna hota hai.

Aadhaar se linked mobile number par OTP aayega. OTP verify hone ke baad Aadhaar authentication complete hota hai.

Uske baad request process hoti hai.

e-PAN available hone par use portal se download kiya ja sakta hai.

Yahan ek important baat hai—Instant e-PAN ke liye eligible hona zaroori hai. Sirf Aadhaar hone se har person automatically Instant e-PAN ke liye eligible nahi ho jata.

Instant e-PAN Ke Liye Kya-Kya Chahiye?

Generally aapko:

  • Valid Aadhaar
  • Aadhaar se linked active mobile number
  • Pehle se PAN allot nahi hua hona
  • Applicable eligibility conditions complete karna

zaroori hota hai.

Agar aapke Aadhaar mein mobile number linked nahi hai, to OTP-based authentication mein problem aayegi.

Aur agar aapke naam par already PAN hai, to new Instant e-PAN ke liye apply nahi karna chahiye.

PAN Banwane Mein Kitna Paisa Lagta Hai?

New PAN card banane ka kharcha is baat par depend karta hai ki aap kaun sa tarika chun rahe hain aur card kahan deliver karwana hai:

  • Instant e-PAN: Agar aap eligible hain aur sirf digital e-PAN chahiye, toh ye service bilkul free hai.
  • Physical PAN Card (India Address): Authorized platforms (Protean ya UTIITSL) ke zariye physical PAN card ke liye apply karne par standard fee ₹107 (taxes included) lagti hai.
  • Physical PAN Card (Foreign Address): Agar card kisi foreign address par mangwana hai, toh iski standard fee ₹1,011 (plus applicable taxes) hoti hai.

Kisi bhi unauthorized agent ko extra fees dene se bachne ke liye hamesha official portal par hi payment karein.

Regular PAN Card Kaise Banaye

India mein PAN-related services ke liye Protean aur UTIITSL jaise authorized providers available hain.

Application ke दौरान aapko apni basic information fill karni hoti hai.

Isme naam, date of birth, address, parent details, contact information aur other required details aa sakti hain.

Application mode ke according documents, photograph aur signature ki requirement bhi ho sakti hai.
