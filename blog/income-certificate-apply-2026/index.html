Income Certificate Apply Kaise Kare 2026 – Bihar Aay Praman Patra Online Full Guide

Posted on: 16 April 2026

Introduction

Agar aap Bihar ke rehne wale hain aur aapko scholarship, government scheme, ya kisi bhi tarah ka financial benefit lena hai, to Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra) aapke liye ek bahut hi important document hai.

Aaj ke time me lagbhag har government kaam me income proof manga jata hai, chahe wo school admission ho, college me fee concession ho, ya kisi yojana ka labh lena ho.

Pehle ke time me logon ko Income Certificate banwane ke liye block ya tehsil office ke kai chakkar lagane padte the. Lekin ab Bihar government ne process ko kaafi easy bana diya hai.

Ab aap ghar baithe mobile ya laptop se online apply kar sakte hain, bina kisi agent ke.

Is detailed guide me hum step-by-step samjhenge ki Income Certificate kaise apply kare, kaun apply kar sakta hai, kaun se documents chahiye, kitne din me banega, aur kaise download kare.

Income Certificate kya hota hai?

Income Certificate ek official government document hota hai jo aapki ya aapke parivar ki annual income ko certify karta hai. Ye certificate State Government ke through issue kiya jata hai.

Iska main purpose hota hai ye prove karna ki aapki income kitni hai, jisse government decide karti hai ki aap kisi scheme ke liye eligible hain ya nahi.

Income Certificate ka use kaha hota hai?

Income Certificate ka use kai important jagahon par hota hai:

  • Scholarship ke liye apply karte waqt
  • School ya college me admission ke samay
  • Government schemes ka benefit lene ke liye
  • Fee concession ya fee reduction ke liye
  • EWS (Economically Weaker Section) certificate banwane ke liye
  • Government jobs me kuch categories ke liye

Agar aap student hain, to ye certificate aapke liye aur bhi important ho jata hai.

Kaun Income Certificate ke liye apply kar sakta hai?

Income Certificate ke liye apply karne ke liye kuch basic eligibility hoti hai:

  • Applicant Bihar ka resident hona chahiye
  • Applicant ke paas valid ID proof hona chahiye
  • Income details sahi honi chahiye

Agar aap student hain to aap apne parents ke income ke basis par apply karte hain.

Required Documents (Jaruri Documents)

Income Certificate banwane ke liye aapko kuch basic documents ki zarurat hoti hai:

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Address Proof
  • Income Proof (salary slip, self declaration ya affidavit)
  • Passport size photo
  • Mobile number

Kai baar logon ko photo upload karte waqt problem hoti hai, jaise size zyada ho jata hai ya format galat hota hai. Is situation me aap Image Resizer Tool ka use karke apni photo ko easily compress aur resize kar sakte hain.

Online Apply Kaise Kare (Step-by-Step Process)

Ab hum sabse important part par aate hain – Income Certificate ka online application process.

Step 1: Official Website open kare

Sabse pehle aapko Bihar ka official RTPS portal open karna hoga:

https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in/

Ye ek government website hai jahan se aap kai tarah ke certificates ke liye apply kar sakte hain.

Step 2: Apply Online par click kare

Website open karne ke baad homepage par aapko “Apply Online” ka option milega. Is par click kare.

Step 3: Service select kare

Ab aapko department choose karna hoga:

  • General Administration Department select kare
  • Fir “Issuance of Income Certificate” par click kare

Step 4: Level choose kare

Aapko 3 option milenge:

  • Block Level
  • Sub Division Level
  • District Level

Agar aap first time apply kar rahe hain to Block Level choose karna sabse easy aur fast hota hai.

Step 5: Application Form fill kare

Ab aapko form fill karna hoga. Isme aapko ye details bharni hongi:

  • Applicant ka naam
  • Father/Mother ka naam
  • Address
  • Income details
  • Mobile number

Dhyan rahe ki sab details bilkul sahi honi chahiye. Galat information dene par application reject ho sakta hai.

Step 6: Documents upload kare

Ab aapko apne documents upload karne honge:

  • Photo upload kare
  • Income proof upload kare

Agar photo upload nahi ho raha hai to aap Image Resizer ka use karke size reduce kar sakte hain.

Step 7: Final submit kare

Sab details bharne ke baad ek baar preview check kare. Agar sab sahi hai to form submit kar de.

Submit karne ke baad aapko ek Application Number milega. Is number ko save karke rakhe kyunki ye future me kaam aayega.

Application Status kaise check kare?

Aap apna application status easily check kar sakte hain:

  • Website open kare
  • “Track Application Status” par click kare
  • Application number enter kare
  • Status check kare

Income Certificate kitne din me ban jata hai?

Generally Income Certificate 7 se 15 din ke andar ban jata hai. Kabhi-kabhi verification process ke wajah se thoda delay bhi ho sakta hai.

Income Certificate download kaise kare?

Jab aapka certificate approve ho jata hai, tab aap use online download kar sakte hain:

  • Website open kare
  • Application number dale
  • Download option par click kare
  • PDF save kar le

Kya Income Certificate free hai?

Haan, Bihar me Income Certificate banana bilkul free hai. Agar koi paisa maangta hai to wo galat hai.

Offline Apply ka option

Agar aap online apply nahi kar pa rahe hain to aap offline bhi apply kar sakte hain:

  • CSC center par jaakar
  • RTPS counter par jaakar

Waha aapka form fill karke apply kar diya jayega.
