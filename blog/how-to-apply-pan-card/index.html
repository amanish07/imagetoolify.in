How to Apply PAN Card Online – Complete Guide (Step-by-Step)

Posted on: 12 March 2026

Introduction

PAN Card (Permanent Account Number) India ka ek bahut important financial document hai. Aaj ke time me agar aap bank account open karna chahte hain, income tax return file karna chahte hain, mutual fund me investment karna chahte hain ya koi bada financial transaction karna chahte hain to PAN card hona almost mandatory ho gaya hai.

PAN card ek 10 digit ka alphanumeric number hota hai jo Income Tax Department of India ke dwara issue kiya jata hai. Ye number har individual, company ya financial entity ke liye unique hota hai aur lifetime ke liye valid rehta hai.

Digital India ke time me ab PAN card banana bahut easy ho gaya hai. Aap ghar baithe online PAN card apply kar sakte hain aur kuch hi dino me aapko physical PAN card ya e-PAN mil jata hai.

Is detailed guide me hum aapko batayenge:

  • PAN card kya hota hai
  • PAN card kyu zaruri hai
  • PAN card ke benefits
  • PAN card online kaise apply kare
  • PAN card banane ke liye documents
  • PAN card fees kitni hai
  • PAN card kitne din me milta hai
  • PAN card status kaise check kare

Agar aap PAN card banane ki planning kar rahe hain to ye article aapke liye complete step-by-step guide hai.

PAN Card Kya Hota Hai

PAN card ka full form Permanent Account Number hota hai. Ye ek unique identification number hota hai jo India ke Income Tax Department ke dwara issue kiya jata hai.

PAN number me total 10 characters hote hain:

Example format: 

ABCDE1234F

Isme:

  • First 5 letters – alphabets
  • Next 4 digits – numbers
  • Last character – alphabet

Ye number lifetime ke liye same rehta hai. Agar aap address change karte hain ya city change karte hain tab bhi PAN number change nahi hota.

PAN card ka main purpose hai:

  • ✔ Financial transactions ko track karna
  • ✔ Tax evasion ko rokna
  • ✔ Identity verification

PAN Card Kyu Zaruri Hai

Aaj ke digital financial system me PAN card bahut important ho gaya hai. Kai financial aur legal processes me PAN card mandatory hota hai.

PAN card ki zarurat in situations me padti hai:

  • Bank account open karne ke liye
  • Income tax return file karne ke liye
  • Mutual fund investment ke liye
  • Stock market trading ke liye
  • Property purchase ke liye
  • Loan apply karne ke liye
  • Credit card apply karne ke liye

Agar aapke paas PAN card nahi hai to kai financial services aapko available nahi hoti.

Isliye agar aap 18+ age ke ho gaye hain to PAN card banwana ek smart decision hota hai.

PAN Card Ke Benefits

PAN card ke kai important benefits hote hain jo financial life ko easy banate hain.

1. Bank Account Open Karna

India me bank account open karte waqt PAN card identity verification ke liye use hota hai.

2. Income Tax Return File Karna

Agar aap income tax return file karte hain to PAN number mandatory hota hai.

3. Loan Aur Credit Card

Bank se personal loan, home loan ya credit card lene ke liye PAN card zaruri hota hai.

4. Investment

Stock market aur mutual fund investment me PAN card compulsory hota hai.

5. Property Transactions

Property purchase ya sale ke time bhi PAN card use hota hai.

6. Financial Transparency

Government PAN number ke through financial transactions track kar sakti hai jisse tax fraud kam hota hai.

PAN Card Online Apply Kaise Kare

Aaj ke time me PAN card apply karna bahut easy ho gaya hai. Aap online PAN card application kuch simple steps me complete kar sakte hain.

Step 1 – Official Website Open Kare

Sabse pehle PAN card application portal open kare.

India me PAN card apply karne ke liye 2 major portals hote hain:

  • NSDL
  • UTIITSL

Step 2 – Form 49A Fill Kare

Indian citizens ke liye Form 49A fill karna hota hai.

Is form me aapko details fill karni hoti hain:

  • Name
  • Father name
  • Date of birth
  • Address
  • Mobile number
  • Email ID

Important Warning: PAN card form fill karte waqt First Name aur Last Name bilkul sahi likhna bahut zaruri hai. Agar spelling mistake ho gayi to baad me PAN card correction karna padega jisme extra time aur fees lag sakti hai. Isliye form submit karne se pehle apna naam carefully verify kare.

Step 3 – Documents Upload Kare

Application process me aapko kuch documents upload karne hote hain.

Jaise:

  • Identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Date of birth proof

Step 4 – Photo Aur Signature Upload Kare

Online application me passport size photo aur signature upload karna hota hai.

Photo size 50kb or signature size 20kb se jyada nahi hona chahiye.

Step 5 – Application Fee Pay Kare

Application complete karne ke liye aapko PAN card processing fee pay karni hoti hai.

India ke andar PAN card apply karne ka charge generally ₹107 ke around hota hai.

Payment options:

  • UPI
  • Debit card
  • Credit card
  • Net banking

Step 6 – Form Submit Kare

Form submit karne ke baad aapko ek acknowledgement number milta hai.

Is number se aap PAN card application status track kar sakte hain.

PAN Card Fees Kitni Hai

PAN card apply karne ke liye government ek small processing fee charge karti hai.

Approx fees:

  • India me – ₹107
  • Foreign address – ₹1017

Ye fees time ke saath change ho sakti hai.

PAN Card Kitne Din Me Milta Hai

PAN card apply karne ke baad processing time generally 10–15 working days hota hai.

Timeline usually aisa hota hai:

• Application processing – 3–5 days

• PAN generation – 5–7 days

• Delivery – 7–10 days

Agar aap e-PAN choose karte hain to kabhi kabhi 24–48 hours me bhi PAN mil jata hai.

e-PAN Card Kya Hota Hai

e-PAN card ek digital version of PAN card hota hai.

Ye PDF format me milta hai aur isme:

• PAN number

• Name

• DOB

• QR code

sab included hota hai.

e-PAN ko aap mobile ya computer me save kar sakte hain.

PAN Card Status Kaise Check Kare

PAN card apply karne ke baad aap application status easily check kar sakte hain.

Steps:

1. PAN status portal open kare

2. Acknowledgement number enter kare

3. Captcha fill kare

4. Status check kare

Aapko pata chal jayega ki:

• Application under process hai

• PAN generate ho gaya hai

• PAN dispatch ho gaya hai

PAN Card Correction Kaise Kare

Kabhi kabhi PAN card me spelling mistake ya address mistake ho jata hai.

Is situation me aap PAN correction form fill kar sakte hain.

Correction ke liye:

1. PAN correction form open kare

2. Correct details fill kare

3. Documents upload kare

4. Fees pay kare

Uske baad updated PAN card issue ho jata hai.

PAN Card Aur Aadhaar Link Karna

Government ne PAN aur Aadhaar linking mandatory kar diya hai.

Agar PAN Aadhaar se link nahi hai to:

• ITR file nahi kar sakte

• PAN inactive ho sakta hai

Isliye PAN banane ke baad Aadhaar link karna zaruri hai.

PAN Card Security Tips

PAN card ek important document hai isliye iski security important hai.

Important Tips:

✔ PAN number kisi unknown person ko share na kare

✔ Fake websites par PAN details fill na kare

✔ PAN card ka misuse hone se bachaye

FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

PAN card banane ke liye minimum age kya hai?

Technically PAN card kisi bhi age me ban sakta hai. Lekin normally adults ke liye banaya jata hai.

PAN card banane me kitna paisa lagta hai?

India me PAN card banane ka charge approx ₹107 hota hai.

PAN card kitne din me milta hai?

Usually 10–15 working days me PAN card deliver ho jata hai.

Kya Aadhaar se PAN card ban sakta hai?

Haan, Aadhaar based e-PAN application se PAN card bahut fast generate ho jata hai.

Kya ek aadmi ke do PAN card ho sakte hain?

Nahi. Ek individual ke paas sirf ek PAN card allowed hota hai.
