PAN Card Kya Hota Hai?

PAN ka full form Permanent Account Number hota hai. Yeh ek unique 10 digit ka number hota hai jo Income Tax Department issue karta hai. Yeh number har individual ke liye alag hota hai aur lifetime valid rehta hai.

Aaj ke time me PAN card ek basic financial document ban chuka hai. Agar aap future me koi bhi financial kaam karna chahte ho, to PAN card hona almost compulsory ho jata hai.

  • Salary receive karne ke liye
  • Loan aur credit card ke liye
  • Mutual fund aur share market investment
