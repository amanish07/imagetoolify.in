🏠 Bihar Residence Certificate (Niwas Praman Patra) Kaise Apply Kare 2026 – Easy Guide

Posted on: 16 April 2026

Agar aap Bihar ke rehne wale hain, to Niwas Praman Patra (Residence Certificate) aapke liye ek bahut hi important document hai. Simple language me bole to ye ek proof hota hai ki aap Bihar ke permanent resident hain.

Chahe aapko government job ke liye apply karna ho, scholarship lena ho ya kisi scheme ka benefit lena ho — har jagah ye certificate kaam aata hai.

Good news ye hai ki ab aapko kisi office ke chakkar lagane ki zarurat nahi hai. Aap ghar baithe hi online apply kar sakte hain 👇

🚀 Apply Now on Official Portal

🧾 Niwas Praman Patra Kya Hota Hai?

Niwas Praman Patra basically ek government document hai jo ye batata hai ki aap Bihar ke permanent resident ho.

Iska use bahut jagah hota hai, jaise:

  • Sarkari naukri me
  • School ya college admission me
  • Scholarship ke liye
  • Government schemes ka benefit lene ke liye

Agar aap Bihar me rehte hain, to ye certificate aapke paas hona almost zaruri hai.

🎯 Ye Certificate Kyu Zaruri Hai?

Ab sawal aata hai ki ye itna important kyu hai?

Simple si baat hai — government ko proof chahiye hota hai ki aap us state ke resident ho. Tabhi aapko state level benefits milte hain.

📌 Kuch main uses:

  • Government jobs me domicile proof
  • Scholarship forms me
  • Ration card ya anya yojana me
  • Identity verification ke liye

📊 Bihar Me Residence Certificate Ke Types

Bihar me ye certificate 3 level par banta hai:

  1. Block Level (Aanchal)
  2. Sub-Division Level (SDO)
  3. District Level (DM)

👉 Agar aap first time apply kar rahe hain, to Block Level se start kare. Ye sabse easy aur fast hota hai.

👤 Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai?

Agar aap:

  • Bihar ke permanent resident hain
  • Aapka address Bihar ka hai
  • Aur aapke paas valid ID proof hai

To aap easily online apply kar sakte hain bina kisi problem ke.

📄 Required Documents (Jaruri Documents)

Apply karte waqt aapko kuch basic documents chahiye honge:

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Voter ID (optional but useful)
  • Passport size photo
  • Address proof
  • Self declaration

👉 Important Tip: Photo upload karte waqt size issue aata hai.

🌐 Online Apply Kaise Kare (Step-by-Step)

Chaliye ab sabse important part — apply kaise karna hai 👇

🔹 Step 1: Website Open Kare

Sabse pehle ye official website open kare:

👉 https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in/

🔹 Step 2: Apply Online Par Click Kare

Homepage par aapko “Apply Online” ka option milega. Us par click karna hai.

🔹 Step 3: Service Select Kare

  • “General Administration Department” choose kare
  • Fir “Issuance of Residential Certificate” par click kare

🔹 Step 4: Level Choose Kare

Aapko 3 option dikhenge:

  • Block Level
  • Sub-Division Level
  • District Level

👉 Beginners ke liye Block Level best hai.

🔹 Step 5: Form Fill Kare

Ab aapko form fill karna hoga:

  • Naam
  • Father/Mother ka naam
  • Address
  • Mobile number
  • Aadhaar details

👉 Dhyan rakhe ki sab info bilkul sahi ho.

🔹 Step 6: Documents Upload Kare

  • Apni photo upload kare
  • ID proof upload kare

👉 Agar photo upload nahi ho raha, to pehle resize kar le.

🔹 Step 7: Final Submit Kare

  • Ek baar preview check kare
  • Sab sahi ho to submit kar de

Submit ke baad aapko ek Application Number mil jayega — ise save karke rakhe.

⏱️ Kitne Din Me Ban Jata Hai?

Usually Niwas Praman Patra 7 se 15 din ke andar ban jata hai.

Kabhi-kabhi thoda delay bhi ho sakta hai, to tension lene ki zarurat nahi hai.

💰 Kya Isme Fees Lagti Hai?

Nahi ❌ Ye service Bihar me bilkul free hai.

Agar koi paisa maang raha hai to wo galat hai.

📊 Application Status Kaise Check Kare?

Aap apna status easily check kar sakte hain:

  1. Website open kare
  2. “Track Application Status” par click kare
  3. Application number dale
  4. Status check kare

📥 Certificate Download Kaise Kare?

Jab aapka certificate approve ho jaye:

  1. Website open kare
  2. Application number dale
  3. Download option par click kare
  4. PDF save kar le

🏢 Offline Apply Ka Option

Agar aap online apply nahi kar pa rahe hain, to:

  • Aap CSC center ya RTPS counter ja sakte hain
  • Waha se bhi apply ho jayega

💡 Important Tips

  • Photo size 100–300 KB rakhe
  • Form jaldi fill kare (timeout hota hai)
  • Sahi details dale
  • Mobile number active rakhe

🌐 RTPS Bihar Portal Kya Hai?

RTPS Bihar ek government portal hai jahan se aap ghar baithe:

  • Caste certificate
  • Income certificate
  • Residence certificate

🤔 FAQs

❓ Q1: Kitne din me certificate milta hai?

👉 7–15 din me

❓ Q2: Kya ye free hai?

👉 Haan, bilkul free hai

❓ Q3: Mobile se apply kar sakte hain?

👉 Haan, easily

❓ Q4: Validity kitni hoti hai?

👉 Normally lifetime (jab tak address change na ho)
