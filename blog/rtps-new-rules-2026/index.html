Bihar RTPS New Rules 2026 – Complete Guide

Posted on: 20 March 2026

RTPS kya hai

Aaj ke time me Bihar me RTPS (Right to Public Service) ka use karke log bahut important certificates banwate hain, jaise:

  • Caste Certificate (Jati Praman Patra)
  • Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra)
  • Residence Certificate (Niwas/Residential Certificate)

Ye documents bahut hi zaroori hote hain government schemes, scholarship, job, admission, aur kai official kaam ke liye.

Lekin 2026 me Bihar government ne RTPS system me bada change (major update) kiya hai. Ab pehle jaise sirf Aadhaar card se certificate banana possible nahi hai. Naye rules ke according ab process thoda strict aur transparent ho gaya hai.

Is article me hum har ek new rule ko detail me samjhenge, simple Hinglish me, taki tumhe clearly samajh aaye aur tum galti na karo jab apply karo.

🚨 RTPS New Rules 2026 – Kya Badla Hai?

Sabse pehle ek simple summary samajh lo:

  • 👉 Aadhaar alone enough nahi hai
  • 👉 Extra documents mandatory ho gaye hain
  • 👉 Family details fill karna compulsory hai
  • 👉 Document nahi hai to bhi option hai (inspection)
  • 👉 Verification ab strict ho gaya hai

Ab chalo ek-ek rule ko detail me samajhte hain 👇

🔴 Rule 1: Sirf Aadhaar Card se Ab Certificate Nahi Banega

✅ Pehle kya hota tha?

Pehle RTPS me aap:

  • Aadhaar card upload karte the
  • Basic details fill karte the
  • Aur certificate generate ho jata tha

❌ Ab kya badla?

Ab:

  • Sirf Aadhaar card dena enough nahi hai
  • Aapko additional documents dene honge

🤔 Kyu change hua?

Government ne ye rule isliye implement kiya:

  • Fake certificates ko rokne ke liye
  • Verification strong karne ke liye

📌 Important Point:

👉 Aadhaar ab bhi zaroori hai, lekin alone sufficient nahi hai

🔴 Rule 2: Application Form Me Full Family Details Fill Karna Hoga

✅ Pehle:

  • Sirf apna naam dalna kaafi tha

❌ Ab:

Aapko application form me ye sab details dena compulsory hai:

  • Applicant ka naam
  • Father ka naam
  • Mother ka naam
  • Husband/Wife ka naam (agar married ho)

💡 Example:

Agar tum married ho, aur spouse ka naam nahi bhara:
👉 Application reject ho sakta hai

🎯 Purpose:

  • Identity clear ho
  • Fake identity cases kam ho

🔴 Rule 3: Document Details Declare Karna Mandatory Hai

Ab sabse important change 👇

✅ Ab aapko batana hoga:

👉 Aapke paas kaun-kaun se documents available hain

📄 Acceptable Documents:

Government ne kuch documents list kiye hain:

  • Khatiyan (land record)
  • Danpatra (gift deed)
  • Land related documents
  • Bhumiheen allocation papers
  • Revenue records
  • Any official land proof

⚠️ Important:

Sirf document upload nahi, balki:
👉 Aapko select bhi karna hoga ki kaunsa document use kar rahe ho

🔴 Rule 4: Agar Documents Nahi Hai to Kya Kare?

Ye sabse interesting aur helpful rule hai 👇

🤔 Situation:

Agar aapke paas:

  • Koi land record nahi
  • Koi revenue document nahi

✅ Solution:

👉 Aap "Site Inspection (Sthal Nirikshan)" option choose kar sakte ho

🔍 Process kya hoga?

  1. Aap application submit karoge
  2. Option select karoge "Documents not available"
  3. Government officer aayega
  4. Ground verification karega
  5. Report banega
  6. Certificate issue hoga

💡 Real Benefit:

👉 Gareeb aur landless log bhi apply kar sakte hain

🔴 Rule 5: Online aur Offline Dono Par Same Rule

Ye rule sab jagah apply hota hai:

🌐 Online:

  • RTPS official portal

🏢 Offline:

  • RTPS counters (block, circle office etc.)

⚠️ Important:

👉 Dono jagah same documents aur rules follow honge

🔴 Rule 6: Fake Certificate Par Strict Action

Government ka main focus hai:

❌ Fake Certificates:

  • Galat jati certificate
  • Fake income certificate
  • Fraud residential proof

✅ Ab kya hoga:

  • Strong verification
  • Cross-checking
  • Physical inspection

🎯 Result:

👉 Sirf genuine logon ko benefit milega

🔴 Rule 7: Document Availability Declare Karna Mandatory

Ab ek aur important change:

✅ Aapko batana hoga:

  • Aapke paas document hai ya nahi

🔄 Do option honge:

  1. Documents available
  2. Documents not available

⚠️ Galat information dene par:

👉 Application reject ho sakta hai
👉 Future me blacklist bhi ho sakte ho

📌 RTPS Apply Karne Se Pehle Dhyan Rakhe

Agar tum 2026 me apply karne wale ho, to ye mistakes bilkul mat karna:

❌ Common Mistakes:

  • Sirf Aadhaar upload karna
  • Father/Mother name skip karna
  • Document option select nahi karna
  • Fake info dena

✅ Correct Approach:

  • Proper documents ready rakho
  • Form carefully fill karo
  • Sahi option select karo

🧾 RTPS Certificate Banane Ka Updated Process (2026)

Step-by-Step:

  1. RTPS portal open karo
  2. Service select karo (Caste/Income/Residence)
  3. Personal details fill karo
  4. Family details add karo
  5. Document option choose karo
  6. Documents upload karo
  7. Submit application
  8. Acknowledgement save karo

🔍 Verification Process Kaise Hoga?

Ab verification thoda strong ho gaya hai:

✅ Methods:

  • Document verification
  • Database cross-check
  • Field inspection (if needed)

⏱️ Time:

Thoda delay ho sakta hai, kyunki:
👉 Process strict ho gaya hai
